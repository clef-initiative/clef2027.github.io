## Welcome to CLEF 2027

The Conference and Labs of the Evaluation Forum (CLEF) brings together researchers, developers, and students interested in information access evaluation and establishing evaluation infrastructures.

CLEF 2027 will take place in **Bucharest, Romania, on September 14–17, 2027**.

The conference combines scientific presentations with evaluation labs focused on information access systems, multilingual and multimodal data, and evaluation methodologies.
