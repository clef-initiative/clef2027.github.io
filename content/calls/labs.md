## Background

[The CLEF Initiative](https://www.clef-initiative.eu/) is a self-organised body whose main mission is to promote research, innovation, and development of information access systems, with an emphasis on multilingual information in different modalities—including text and multimedia—and with various levels of structure. CLEF promotes research and development by providing an infrastructure for:

- Independent evaluation of information access systems.
- Investigation of the use of unstructured, semi-structured, highly structured, and semantically enriched data in information access.
- Creation of reusable test collections for benchmarking.
- Exploration of new evaluation methodologies and innovative ways of using experimental data.
- Discussion of results, comparison of approaches, exchange of ideas, and transfer of knowledge.

## Scope of CLEF Labs

We invite proposals for two types of labs:

1. **Campaign-style evaluation labs** addressing specific information access problems during the twelve-month period preceding the conference. These are similar to traditional CLEF campaign tracks. Topics can be drawn from any domain or task related to information access.
2. **Workshop-style labs** exploring evaluation methodologies, metrics, and processes in information access and closely related fields, such as natural language processing, machine translation, and human-computer interaction.

We strongly recommend that organisers new to the CLEF shared task evaluation format first consider organising a lab workshop to discuss their proposed task, its problem space, and the practicalities of running a shared task.

The CLEF 2027 programme will reserve about half of the conference schedule for lab sessions. Lab organisers will present their overall results in overview presentations during the plenary scientific paper sessions, giving non-participants insights into emerging research directions.

Organisers are expected to arrange separate lab sessions with ample time for general discussion and engagement with all participants, including those who are not presenting campaign results or papers. Sessions should include activities such as panels, demonstrations, and poster sessions, as appropriate.

CLEF welcomes innovative lab proposals. Proposers who are unsure whether their task or proposed format is suitable are encouraged to contact the [CLEF 2027 Lab Chairs](#clef-2027-lab-chairs) at an early stage.

## Proposal Submission

Lab proposals must provide sufficient information to assess their relevance, timeliness, scientific quality, benefits for the research community, and the proposers' ability to coordinate the lab. Each proposal should identify one or more organisers responsible for ensuring its timely execution.

Proposals should be **3–4 pages** long and include the following information:

1. **Title:** The title of the proposed lab.
2. **Topic and goals:** A brief description of the topic and goals, their relevance to CLEF, and their significance for the field.
3. **Usage scenarios and evaluation:** A clear statement of the intended usage scenarios and domain, including the evaluation setup and metrics.
4. **Organisers:** Details of the lab organisers and task chairs responsible for running the tasks, including previous experience organising or participating in evaluation tasks at CLEF or similar campaigns.
5. **Format:** Whether the lab will follow a campaign-style track or a workshop format.
6. **Previous editions or new activity:** State whether the lab continues an existing activity or introduces a new one.

   - **Continuing activities:** Provide statistics from previous years, including participant and run counts for each task. Explain why another edition is needed, list the proposed changes, and discuss the lessons or insights expected.
   - **New activities:** Explain why a new evaluation campaign is needed and how the community would benefit.

7. **Target audience and outreach:** Describe who is expected to participate and how the organisers plan to reach them.
8. **Tasks and datasets:** Describe and motivate each proposed task, providing evidence that it can attract sufficient participation. Describe the datasets, explain how they support the lab's goals, and indicate how they will be shared. A lab may have a single task or several closely related tasks. **More than three tasks require a strong justification** to avoid unnecessary fragmentation.
9. **Session length and structure:** Request a half-day, one-day, or two-day session. Justify the requested duration with a proposed structure, including participant presentations, invited speakers, panels, or other activities.
10. **Campaign organisation:** Identify who will be responsible for each activity. Explain how data will be acquired or created and which tools or methods will be used, including how queries will be created or relevance assessments performed, where applicable. Include any other information relevant to running the lab.
11. **Steering committee, if applicable:** If a steering committee is proposed, include the names, addresses, and homepage links of its prospective members.

## Review Process

Each proposal **submitted by 17 July 2026** will be reviewed by the CLEF 2027 Lab Organising Committee. Acceptance decisions will be emailed to the responsible organiser by **7 August 2026**.

The final length of each lab's conference session will depend on the overall conference organisation and the number of participant submissions received by the lab.

## Advertising Labs at CLEF 2026 and ECIR 2027

Organisers of accepted labs are expected to advertise their labs at both events:

- **CLEF 2026:** 21–24 September 2026, Jena, Germany.
- **ECIR 2027:** 21–25 March 2027, Southampton, UK.

**At least one representative from each lab should attend both events.**

At CLEF 2026, advertising will consist of displaying a poster describing the new lab and announcing it during the closing session.

At ECIR 2027, organisers will submit a lab description for inclusion in the conference proceedings by **5 October 2026** and advertise the lab in a booster session.

## Lab Proposals from Newcomers

If you have not organised a lab before, the CLEF 2027 Lab Organising Committee can offer mentorship, guidance, and feedback on your draft proposal.

To request guidance, email all four [Lab Chairs](#clef-2027-lab-chairs) using this subject tag:
